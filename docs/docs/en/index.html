Haxora, Free HaxBall Client.

Haxora is a free, unofficial HaxBall client for Windows, a desktop app for playing HaxBall. Higher FPS, less delay, a friend list, private messages, goal effects and a pile of things the browser never gave you. Choose the Windows installer or the portable ZIP package.

Features

HaxBall client features: everything you need, in one place.

Instead of a flashy feature list, Haxora focuses on the things you actually use every day.

Performance and play

High FPS

Lift the frame rate cap. The game doesn't slow down when it goes to the background.

Training mode

Practise shooting and passing on your own. Load your own pitch and try any scenario.

Match recording

Record a match and reopen it to watch it back later.

Friends and chat

Friend list and private messages

See who is online and which room they are in, then join them with a single click. Message them without leaving the match; if a friend is offline, your message waits for them.

Message notifications

A message that arrives mid-match shows up in a notification that never covers the pitch. Its position and prominence are up to you.

Muting

Silence the player getting on your nerves, on your screen only. It works in rooms that relay chat through a bot too, and other players are not affected.

Avatar reactions

Bind an emoji to a key; your avatar becomes that emoji while you hold it.

Favourite rooms and room history

The rooms you play in most, plus your room history, always at hand.

Room stats

Track your goals, assists, time played and wins room by room.

Looks and customisation

Themes and looks

Accent colour, ball skin, scoreboard and key bindings, set the way you like them.

Goal effects

Scoring and conceding get their own visual effect on the pitch. Pick a different one for each, or turn them off.

Shareable settings

Turn your looks and key bindings into a code and send it to a friend. Paste a code you receive and their setup opens on your side.

Settings that follow you

With an account, your profile and preferences are waiting on your other computer too.

English / Turkish interface

Pick the language on first run and change it whenever you like. It applies immediately.

Integrations

Spotify controls

Play, pause or skip a track without leaving the match. No alt-tab, no ball lost while you do it.

Discord Rich Presence

While you play, your Discord profile shows that you are on Haxora.

Install Haxora

  • 1. Download Haxora-Setup.exe or Haxora.zip.
  • 2. Run the installer, or extract the ZIP package and open the app.
  • 3. Pick a player name and join a room.

Requires: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

If Windows says it "protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. The warning appears because the file has no code-signing certificate; every unsigned program gets it, and it doesn't mean anything was found in the file. Certificates cost money, so Haxora ships unsigned for now.

You don't need an account to play. An account is only for the friend list, private messages and settings sync.

Is Haxora safe, and what does it keep?

  • Download it here only. The Releases page of the GitHub repository is the one official channel. A "Haxora" file on any other site has nothing to do with the developer.
  • No account required. You don't need to sign in to join a room and play.
  • Your settings stay with you. Themes, key bindings and favourite rooms are stored locally on your machine.
  • Spotify. You connect through Spotify's own sign-in screen, so Haxora never sees your password; it only handles playback (play / pause / skip).
  • It doesn't touch the game itself. Haxora is an independent client built on node-haxball; features like muting apply to your screen only and don't affect other players.

Project page and public releases: Haxora on GitHub.

Changing the language

The interface ships in English and Turkish. Change it on first run, with the TR / EN pill in the top bar of the main menu, or in Settings → General → Language, in the lobby or during a match. It applies immediately; nothing needs restarting.

FAQ

Questions about Haxora and the HaxBall client.

What exactly is Haxora?

An independent, unofficial HaxBall client that lets you open HaxBall from your desktop. Alongside the game it adds a friend list, messaging, stats and customisation.

Does it cost anything, are there ads?

No. Haxora is free and has no ads in it.

Do I need to install anything?

Choose Haxora-Setup.exe to install, or extract Haxora.zip to use the portable version.

Do I have to create an account?

No. An account is only used for the online features: the friend list, private messages and settings sync. You can join rooms and play without signing in.

Does muting affect the other person?

No. Muting applies to your screen only; everyone else keeps seeing that player.

Do I need to connect my Spotify account?

Yes, once, so the controls can work. You connect through Spotify's own sign-in screen, so Haxora never sees your password; it only handles playback.

Is there a Mac or Linux build?

Right now only Windows 64-bit is supported.

How do updates arrive?

New builds are published on the GitHub Releases page, with the changes written in the release notes.

Is it officially connected to HaxBall?

No. Haxora is an independent project with no official affiliation, partnership or endorsement from HaxBall.

Reporting a problem

If something doesn't work, or you have an idea, open an issue on GitHub. It gets sorted much faster if you include:

  • your Haxora version
  • what you were doing, step by step
  • a screenshot, if you have one
